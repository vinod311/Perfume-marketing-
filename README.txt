TRIVENKEM FRAGRANCE FINDER - complete app

UPLOAD TO GITHUB
Upload every file in this folder to your repository (index.html must be in the main folder).
Then: Settings > Pages > Branch: main > / (root) > Save.
Open the app link, then add it to your phone home screen from Chrome.

ASK TAB
- Works offline and free with no setup (searches your catalogue).
- Optional free AI: tap "Turn on free AI", create a free Google key at aistudio.google.com/apikey,
  paste it and tap Save. The key stays on that phone only.
- Tick "Also search Wikipedia" for general knowledge (free, no key needed).
- Free Google limits: about 20 AI answers a day, then a lighter model (about 500 a day).

AFTER AN UPDATE
Close the app fully and reopen it twice. The bottom of the page shows the version (v10).

PRIVACY
No login, no ads, no analytics. My notes (price, stock) are never sent anywhere.
With AI on, the question and recent chat go to Google. Staff must not type customer personal details.
privacy.html is a general template, not legal advice. Replace [your email here] with your email.
